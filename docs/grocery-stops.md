# Grocery stops along the route — Trader Joe's + alternatives

_Researched July 2026 against the day-by-day schedule. Store details change, so
re-check hours the day of. TJ hours below are the listed daily hours at time of
writing._

> **What to buy:** see **`food-plan.md`** for the meal framework + master shopping
> list for the KC → Detroit leg. This doc is *where* to shop; that one is *what*.

## TL;DR

The route is **Trader Joe's–rich** — there's a TJ at or beside almost every major
stop: Denver/Westminster, Kansas City, Cincinnati, NW Indiana, metro Detroit,
Orland Park (IL), Coralville (IA), and Lincoln (NE). The only real gaps are the
rural middle stretches (BV/Salida, Columbia/New Truxton MO, Nashville IN, central
IL, Kearney/Gibbon NE), and those are all well covered by **Hy-Vee** (west half)
and **Meijer** (east half). Practical plan: **big TJ stock-ups at the KC base
(8/12–16) and the 7-night Worlds basecamp (Northville, 8/24), top-ups elsewhere.**

## Chains worth knowing on this route

- **Meijer** — MI, IN, IL, OH. Supercenters with groceries *and* camping/outdoor
  gear, many open late/24h. Your best all-arounder for the whole eastern half
  (Holland, Valparaiso, the Detroit metro). Good for propane, ice, firewood, a
  forgotten chair.
- **Hy-Vee** — IA, NE, KS, MO, IL. Excellent grocery with strong deli/hot food and
  Wahlburgers/market grille inside many — handy when you don't want to cook at camp.
  Covers the western half (Columbia, Lincoln, Kearney, Iowa).
- **Kroger family** — **King Soopers / City Market** (CO), **Dillons** (KS),
  **Gerbes** (MO), **Kroger** (IN/OH/MI). One loyalty account works across all of
  them; fuel points add up over a month of driving.
- **Aldi** — TJ's sister company, in every state on the route. Fastest, cheapest
  stop for camp staples (eggs, produce, snacks) when a TJ isn't nearby.
- **Walmart Supercenter** — the universal fallback, and many locations still allow
  overnight RV/camper parking (call the store first) if you ever need an unplanned
  night on the road.

## Camp-pantry tips for this trip

- **Big shops at the two anchors** (KC 8/12–16, Northville 8/24) keep you out of
  daily grocery runs during the disc-golf leg and Worlds week.
- No sewer hookup at Proud Lake — go easy on things that make lots of dishwater.
- August heat is the main risk on course: TJ/Aldi are cheap for **electrolyte
  drinks, frozen fruit, and easy cooler food**; grab bagged ice at any Meijer/
  Hy-Vee/Casey's on the way to camp.
- **Casey's** (gas stations, everywhere in the Midwest) = reliable ice, coffee,
  and surprisingly good pizza when you roll into camp late.
