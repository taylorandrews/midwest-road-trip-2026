# Food plan — KC → Detroit disc-golf leg (Aug 17–25)

Camp-cooking plan for the 8-day driving leg out of the KC base. The per-day meals
are baked into the trip itself (`site/data/schedule.json` → shows on the phone
guide as a **Meals** section + a 🍴 dinner chip). This doc is the master
**shopping list**, the **meal framework**, and the **prep** it depends on.

Pair with **`grocery-stops.md`** for *where* to buy along the route. The two anchor
shops: **big stock-up at the KC base (8/12–16)** for everything below, then a
**reset shop at the Northville TJ on 8/25** for Worlds week.

## The framework

- **8 breakfasts** at camp: eggs + English muffin + cheese + a chicken sausage.
  (Aug 17 is the *last burrito from home* instead — the camp-breakfast supply
  covers Aug 18–25.)
- **8 lunches**: tuna-salad sandwiches, made 3 days ahead at a time. Two prep
  sessions — **Aug 17** and **Aug 21** — plus two eat-out lunches (Reds game 8/20,
  on the road 8/24). Tigers game covers 8/25.
- **8 dinners**: 2× burgers, 2× pesto tortellini, 2× veggie curry + rice, each
  cooked once and eaten again as leftovers, plus 2 nights of dinner on the road
  (the two long drive days, 8/18 & 8/20).
- **snacks + instant coffee** throughout.

## Shopping list (KC base stock-up, 8/12–16)

**Breakfast**
- 2 dozen eggs
- 8 English muffins (or 6 muffins + 4 slices bread)
- 16 slices cheese — 2 kinds (8 for breakfast, 8 for tuna sandwiches)
- 8 chicken sausages
- 3 jars salsa
- hot sauce

**Lunch (tuna salad)**
- 8 cans tuna
- jar of mayo
- celery (pre-chop before leaving)
- red onion (check — may already have)
- loaf of bread — must be **20 slices** (16 for sandwiches + margin)
- 1 head romaine

**Dinners**
- burgers ×2 nights
- pesto + tortellini ×2 nights
- mixed veggies (or 2 bell peppers + broccoli + zucchini), curry, instant rice,
  small tofu block ×2 nights

**Staples:** snacks, instant coffee.

## Pre-trip (day before departure, at home / KC)

Cook/consume ahead so nothing is wasted before the cooler leg starts:
- **2 burritos** — eat one; **save one for Monday 8/17 AM** (it's that day's breakfast).
- **3 salads** — for the KC days.
- **2 home breakfasts** — just eggs, using up the bread on hand.

## Notes

- **Tuna prep cadence:** 3 servings on 8/17, 3 more on 8/21 — mix cans with mayo +
  pre-chopped celery/red onion, keep cold. Romaine + cheese add crunch at assembly.
- **No sewer hookup at Proud Lake** (see grocery-stops.md) — the make-once/leftover
  dinner pattern keeps dishwater down for the basecamp stretch.
- **Reset on 8/25:** the leg supply runs out at 8/25 breakfast; restock for the
  7-night Worlds basecamp at the Northville Trader Joe's.
