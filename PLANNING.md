# Planning scratchpad

Free-form planning space for the trip. **Type anything here** — it's just a text
file, so edit it in any editor, save, and it's kept (version-controlled, never
lost). Then in the terminal tell Claude **"fold in my planning notes"** (paste any
emails/screenshots too). Claude reads this file + your materials and bakes the
settled stuff into the real trip (`site/data/schedule.json`), which the phone
guide shows.

Nothing here is structured or required — headings are just suggestions. Once an
idea is locked into the trip, you can delete it from here or leave it.
